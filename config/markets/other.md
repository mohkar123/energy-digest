# Other markets context

Lower-priority markets. Surface an item only if it is clearly relevant to load forecasting, settlement or large-load growth, or if it concerns a named partner or customer from the watchlist.

- **Australia:** AEMO (market operator, Integrated System Plan, Electricity Statement of Opportunities, demand forecasting methodology) and the AER (network price determinations, which depend on demand forecasts). Why it matters: closest analogue to NZ for planning methodology (AEMO's published forecasting approach is a common reference for long-term forecasting), rooftop-solar penetration, and potential expansion. Watch for forecasting methodology updates, rooftop-solar and battery rules, and data-centre load assumptions.
- **Ontario, Canada:** IESO (system operator, demand forecast, capacity auctions) and the OEB (rates and distributor regulation). Why it matters: electrification, data-centre demand, and distributor planning rules (such as OEB requirements for distribution system plans and DER integration) are direct drivers. Watch for IESO annual planning outlooks and OEB decisions.
- **European Union:** electricity market design reform (flexibility, capacity mechanisms, demand-response), smart-meter and data-access rules, and national TSO/DSO forecasting obligations. Why it matters: benchmark for settlement reform and flexibility markets; a source of competitor and partner activity. Lower direct relevance unless it concerns half-hourly settlement or load disaggregation.
- **Partnerships:** news about asset-investment-planning partners on the watchlist is relevant, since forecasts feed investment planning.

A signal in these markets is a rule or forecast-methodology change, a named watchlist account or partner in the news, or a major competitor win. Mark any uncertain claim "verify".

## Calendar anchors and reading guidance
- Australia: AEMO publishes its Integrated System Plan and Electricity Statement of Opportunities on fixed annual cycles (verify months); AER determinations follow multi-year regulatory periods.
- Ontario: IESO publishes its annual planning outlook and runs capacity procurements; OEB rate decisions follow distributor filing cycles (verify).
- EU: market design implementation runs through national transposition deadlines (verify dates).
- Treat generic policy commentary as low relevance; prefer items with a rule, a docket, a forecast number or a named organisation.
